# Patterns — RSD Life OS

> Recurring structures and failure modes the book names explicitly. Load when an answer needs the *shape* of a situation rather than a chapter's detail.

## Structures the book names

### 1. Predicament is an asset-restructuring notice (ch 7)
A major difficulty is not punishment — it is the system notifying you that a restructuring is due. The Rebuilding Tool's whole premise: predicament → R (own it) → S (see its Dao/Li) → D (act with the flow) → converted capital. **The bigger the predicament, the bigger the asset it announces** — if, and only if, step 1 is not skipped.

### 2. From external seeking to internal confirmation (ch 6.5)
The awakened person's only behavioral mode: stop searching outward for permission, proof, or rescue; confirm identity first, then act. External seeking is the small self's default; internal confirmation is the sovereign's.

### 3. From using the tool to becoming the tool (ch 7.5)
Mastery's arc: you operate the tools → the tools operate you → you *become* the system, and your life itself is its clearest manifestation. The end state is not proficiency but identity.

### 4. Form first, energy second — always (ch 2)
The Analysis Tool's fixed order: read the observable Li (form, law, pattern), *then* name the Dao (energy, purpose) that produced it. Reversing the order is not insight but projection — inventing an energy story to fit a form you already decided on.

### 5. Survival first, altruism after (ch 2.3)
The First Law is the precondition, not one option among many: stay alive, *then* practice the Heavenly Way. Altruism that ignores survival destroys the carrier and violates the First Law. The meaning of a carrier's existence: first stay alive, second become.

### 6. Wu Wei is not inaction (ch 2.3, ch 3.3)
Not acting recklessly — but toward what violates the Heavenly Way you must act, step in, and protect. Altruism is not indiscriminate kindness. The system is not passive; it is precisely aimed.

### 7. Family is the ferry boat (ch 10.2)
Home is the energy foundation — the ferry that carries you across, the aircraft carrier behind your sorties. "When the family is harmonious, everything prospers" is not about house size or money but about the energy backing you can launch from.

### 8. Wealth is the energy receipt (ch 12.1)
Wealth is the "Energy Voucher" of altruistic value already delivered — the receipt, not the goal. Chase the value delivered; the voucher arrives as settlement per the Third Law.

### 9. The causal chain, in order, never reversed (ch 12.2)
Selfless Altruism → Trust Asset → Vibrational Kin → Caravan → wealth returns. Each link is the next one's only legitimate source. Skipping to the last link (wealth first) inverts the chain and produces exactly the "money is hard to earn" predicament the chain exists to solve.

### 10. Everything is there for you to use (Fortune Note)
"All things are prepared for me" is not an entitlement to consume — it is the RSD use-logic: things are for *use*, and using them well (including sharing the book) is the completing act, not a violation of non-attachment.

## Failure modes the book names

| Failure | What it looks like | The correction |
|---|---|---|
| **Slogan awakening** | Repeating "I am the Sovereign Ruler" with zero change in next action | Awakening is measured by the next act, not the recital |
| **Charging as avoidance** | Recharging to feel better instead of to be able to act | Charge → then act; feeling better is the side effect, not the purpose |
| **Observation as prediction** | Using the Observation Tool to forecast outcomes | The book explicitly forbids prediction (ch 8.4); redirect to what can be done now |
| **Observation as medicine** | Treating the tool as a substitute for clinical care | Health is paramount; see a clinician. The tool works at the awareness level |
| **Navigation as self-scoring** | Tallying +1/−1 to feel good or bad about yourself | The marking exists for course correction, not for self-evaluation |
| **Skipping R** | Jumping to insight (S) or action (D) without owning the situation | Insight without ownership is complaint; action without insight is repetition |
| **Projection** | Deciding the form first, then inventing the energy story | Run the lens in order: form first, energy second |
| **Moralizing** | Presenting the laws as virtues to admire | The laws are mechanisms, not sermons. If the answer reads like preaching, it has failed |
| **Dogmatic splitting** | Quoting one law while discarding the other two | The three laws are one instrument; never cite one against another |
| **Reversing cause and effect** | Treating wealth as the cause of altruism instead of its effect | The chain runs one way only (see pattern 9) |
| **Indiscriminate kindness** | "Altruism" that harms the system it claims to serve | Altruism advances the whole system's harmony; pushover altruism is a −1 |

## Decision shortcuts

- **"What should I do?"** → Is this a predicament (Rebuilding Tool), a judgment (Analysis Tool), a relationship (ch 11), money (ch 12), body/habit (Charging/Observation), or direction (ch 10)? Load at most one or two chapters.
- **"Why does this keep happening?"** → Run the Analysis Tool on the pattern itself: what Li (structure) repeats, what Dao (energy) sustains it.
- **"How do I decide?"** → Test against the three laws in order: survival, behavior, cause and effect.
- **Every answer ends in action** — a question to ask, a procedure step, or a +1 calibration. Insight alone is an incomplete answer in this system.
