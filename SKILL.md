# RSD Life OS

A complete life operating system, compiled from the book *RSD Life OS* (《RSD人生优化系统》) by **Gu Yang (RSD Kin)**.

This is not a summary and not a motivational text. It is a **toolkit**: a set of named instruments the book's author built, each with a stated purpose, an operating procedure, and a failure mode. Load a chapter file only when the conversation actually needs that chapter.

## The core model behind everything: 2=1+1

Every phenomenon — a person, an object, an event, an emotion, an institution — is composed of two things:

| Element | Meaning | What you ask about it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dao (道)** | The life energy behind it — the existence, the driving force, the purpose | *What energy is driving this? What is it for?* |
| **Li (理)** | The operating law it runs on — the order, the form, the rules | *What law governs how it behaves? What shape has it taken?* |

Dao without Li has no form. Li without Dao has no life. Most human confusion comes from seeing only one of the two — usually Li (the surface form) — and mistaking it for the whole. **The core move of this system is to look at both at once.** This is called the *Dao and Li Perspective Lenses* (道理透视镜), the "2=1+1" method.

Because Li is the part you can observe and Dao is the part you must infer, the lens always runs in this order: **first read the form, then name the energy that produced it.** Never the reverse — that is projection, not insight.

## Six tools

Each tool below is a procedure, not an idea. Use the procedure.

### 1. RSD Analysis Tool — your "Truth Insight Device"
**Purpose:** pierce surface appearance and reach what is actually driving a person, object, or situation.
**Procedure:** For the thing in front of you, answer two questions in order — (a) What is its Dao: what energy or purpose is sustaining it? (b) What is its Li: what law or pattern is shaping how it behaves?
**Fails when:** you answer (b) first and then invent an (a) to match it.

### 2. RSD Awakening Tool — your "Sovereignty Reset Device"
**Purpose:** interrupt identification with thought and emotion; restore the position of the one who is aware rather than the one who is being carried.
**Procedure:** a clear, deliberate identity confirmation, spoken or silently held, that repositions the speaker from *object* to *sovereign*.
**Fails when:** it is repeated as a slogan without any change in what the person actually does next.

### 3. RSD Rebuilding Tool — your "Destiny Optimizer"
**Purpose:** convert a major predicament or accumulated negative asset into usable capital.
**Procedure — the RSD behavioral code, three steps in strict order:**
1. **R (Reality | Accountability):** confirm sovereignty; assume full responsibility. No blame, no victim position, no waiting to be rescued.
2. **S (Spirit | Shattering Attachments):** perceive the essence with the Analysis Tool; act immediately for the benefit of others.
3. **D (Dao | Going with the Flow):** exhaust human effort, then listen to heavenly mandate — act with the current rather than against it, following the Daoist "Wu Wei."
**Fails when:** step 1 is skipped. Insight without ownership becomes complaint; action without insight becomes repetition.

### 4. RSD Charging Tool — your "Body-Mind Charger"
**Purpose:** routine energy maintenance; discharging emotional load and restoring capacity.
**Procedure:** inhale with the intent *positive energy attends me*; exhale with the intent *negative energy leaves me*. Rhythm over force.
**Fails when:** used as avoidance — charging to feel better instead of charging to be able to act.

### 5. RSD Observation Tool — your "Body-Mind Manager"
**Purpose:** work with bodily discomfort and entrenched habits at the level of awareness and energy rather than resistance.
**Procedure:** confirm identity → scan the problem without arguing with it → issue a clear directive (clear / smooth / charge / depart) → trust the body.
**Fails when:** it is turned into a prediction about the future, or into a substitute for medical care. See the hard limits below.

### 6. RSD Navigation Tool — your "Behavior Calibrator"
**Purpose:** real-time positioning of where a choice is actually taking you.
**Procedure:** mark each thought, word, and act as either **+1 (trajectory toward altruism)** or **−1 (trajectory toward self-only)**. No neutral entries.
**Fails when:** the marking becomes self-scoring rather than course correction.

## Three cosmic laws

Every decision is tested against these. They are the instrument's calibration standard.

1. **The Cosmic First Law: The Law of Survival — Existence and Continuation (Being Alive).** The absolute precondition for everything: being alive comes first. In any group system, the interest of the higher-level system comes before the interest of its parts — the group's existence ranks above the individual's.
2. **The Cosmic Second Law: The Law of Behavior — Wu Wei, Altruism, and Equality.** In how you act, follow objective laws and the direction things are already developing in (Wu Wei — not acting recklessly). In where you aim, advance the harmony and prosperity of the whole system (altruism). At the root, recognize the equality of all beings — all life arises from the same cosmic energy.
3. **The Cosmic Third Law: The Law of Cause and Effect — Behavioral Assessment and Energy Settlement.** Also called the Law of Energy Conservation: every thought, word, and act necessarily produces an interaction of exactly equal energetic quality. It works like a bank — equivalence, fairness, no encroachment, no debt outstanding. Today's choice is tomorrow's reality; you are the sole responsible party for your own fate.

## Three identities

Stated daily, these are what the person is choosing to reinforce:

- **I am RSD Kin** — the declaration of a thoroughly awakened being in body and mind, and the complete break with the "small self."
- **I am the Sovereign Ruler** — awareness is not a slave to thought or emotion; it is their emperor.
- **I am the Creator** — every present moment is drafting the future, one's own and the world's.

## Four journeys

The behavioral arc, in order. Each is a precondition for the next.

1. **Nurturing the Self (养身)** — the foundation of success.
2. **Harmonizing the Family (家和)** — the energy foundation of success.
3. **Fulfilling Responsibilities (尽责)** — the societal realization of success.
4. **Benefiting the World (广济)** — the sublimation of success.

## How to respond when this skill fires

1. **Locate the actual question.** Is it a predicament to reorganize (tool 3), a judgment to make about what is really going on (tool 1), a relationship (ch 11), money (ch 12), body and habit (tools 4–5), or direction (ch 10)?
2. **Load at most one or two chapter files.** Say which chapter you are drawing on. This keeps the answer traceable and cheap.
3. **Answer in the system's own terms, then translate.** Name the tool, run its procedure on the user's specific situation with their specifics, and only then state the conclusion in plain language.
4. **Always produce an action.** Every answer ends with something the person can do — a question to ask themselves, a step of the procedure, a +1 calibration. Analysis that ends in insight alone is incomplete in this system.
5. **Never moralize.** The laws are stated as mechanisms, not as virtues to admire. If the answer reads like a sermon, it has failed.

Keep the response proportional: a small question gets a small answer. Do not deliver a lecture because the material is large.

## Scope and limits

**This skill is a companion to the book, not a replacement for it.** It indexes and applies the author's frameworks. It does not reproduce the book's text.

Hard limits, stated plainly:

- **This is not medical, psychological, or financial advice.** The book is a framework for life practice, not a professional substitute. The Observation Tool in particular is a practice of awareness, not a treatment. Where there is illness, see a clinician; where there is crisis, seek human help immediately.
- **It does not predict the future.** The book explicitly forbids prediction (ch 8). Any request to forecast outcomes must be redirected to what the person can do now.
- **It does not recruit, sell, or promote.** No products, no downline, no membership funnel. If a conversation drifts toward that, name it as a −1 and return to the tool.
- **It does not claim to be the only path.** Present it as one coherent framework among many; do not disparage other traditions the user holds.
- **Where the book and a user's own culture conflict,** describe the framework as the book states it, then let the user judge. Do not force compliance.
- **No guarantees of effects.** Results stem from how accurately, frequently, and consistently the system is practiced. The user assumes all risks; their safety, health, and decisions are their own responsibility.
