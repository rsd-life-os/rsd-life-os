# Glossary — RSD Life OS

> Every named term in the system, defined in one line. English names are authoritative (from the published edition); Chinese in parentheses for reference. Load this file when a term needs defining, not preemptively.

## The Six Tools

| Term | One-line definition |
|---|---|
| **RSD Analysis Tool** (《RSD分析法》) | Your "Truth Insight Device." The 2=1+1 method: read a thing's Li (form/law) first, then name the Dao (energy/purpose) behind it. The system's core cognitive instrument. |
| **RSD Awakening Tool** (《RSD唤醒法》) | Your "Sovereignty Reset Device." A deliberate identity confirmation that repositions you from object of thought/emotion to sovereign of them. |
| **RSD Rebuilding Tool** (《RSD重组法》) | Your "Destiny Optimizer." Converts predicament into capital via the R→S→D behavioral code. |
| **RSD Charging Tool** (《RSD充电法》) | Your "Body-Mind Charger." Breath-and-focus routine that clears emotional load and restores energy. |
| **RSD Observation Tool** (《RSD观测法》) | Your "Body-Mind Manager." Works with bodily discomfort and habits at the level of awareness and energy — never as prediction, never as medical substitute. |
| **RSD Navigation Tool** (《RSD导航仪》) | Your "Behavior Calibrator." Marks every thought/word/act +1 (altruistic) or −1 (self-only). No neutral entries. |

## The Core Model

| Term | One-line definition |
|---|---|
| **Dao (道)** | The life energy behind a thing — its existence, driving force, purpose. The part you must infer. |
| **Li (理)** | The operating law a thing runs on — its order, form, rules. The part you can observe. |
| **2=1+1** | Every thing = Dao + Li. The formula behind the Analysis Tool. |
| **Dao and Li Perspective Lenses** (道理透视镜) | The lens that sees both Dao and Li at once; always run form-first, energy-second. |
| **Shi (momentum)** (势) | The directional flow of a situation that Wu Wei aligns with. |

## The Three Cosmic Laws

| Term | One-line definition |
|---|---|
| **Cosmic First Law: Law of Survival — Existence and Continuation** (宇宙第一法则：生存法则) | Being alive comes first; in any group system the higher-level system's interest precedes its parts'. |
| **Cosmic Second Law: Law of Behavior — Wu Wei, Altruism, and Equality** (宇宙第二法则：行为法则) | Act by objective laws (Wu Wei), aim at the whole system's harmony (altruism), recognize all beings' equality. |
| **Cosmic Third Law: Law of Cause and Effect — Behavioral Assessment and Energy Settlement** (宇宙第三法则：因果法则) | Also called the Law of Energy Conservation: every cause produces an exactly equal effect; it works like a bank. |
| **Wu Wei (无为)** | Not acting recklessly — following objective laws and the direction things already develop in. Not inaction. |
| **Selfless Altruism** (无我利他) | Offering your value in service without clinging to return. Survival first, altruism after. |
| **The Way of Heaven, Benefiting Without Harming** (天之道，利而不害) | The root behavioral law: Dao's essence is to give, not take. |
| **The Way of Humanity, Acting Without Contending** (人之道，为而不争) | The supreme posture: actively create value without contending for credit, fame, or result. |
