# Cheatsheet — RSD Life OS

> One page. Decision tables and one-line rules. Load this first when you need to act fast.

| What the person brings | Reach for |
|---|---|
| A stuck situation, repeated failure, a crisis | **Rebuilding Tool** — R→S→D (ch 7) |
| "What's really going on here?" | **Analysis Tool** — 2=1+1 (ch 2) |
| Anxiety, identification with thoughts/emotions | **Awakening Tool** — identity confirmation (ch 6) |
| Drained, reactive, can't sleep, emotional load | **Charging Tool** — breath routine (ch 8) |
| Bodily discomfort, entrenched habit | **Observation Tool** — directive sequence (ch 8) |
| A choice, a temptation, "is this okay?" | **Navigation Tool** — +1/−1 (ch 9) |
| Relationship conflict, communication | ch 11 |
| Money, career, business model | ch 12 |
| "What's my direction in life?" | Four Journeys (ch 10) |
| Environment, space, "bad energy" at home/work | ch 13 |

## The six tools on one card

| # | Tool | Procedure in one line | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Analysis** | Read the form (Li), then name the energy (Dao) | you invent the energy first |
| 2 | **Awakening** | Deliberate identity confirmation: object → sovereign | it stays a slogan |
| 3 | **Rebuilding** | R (own it) → S (see it) → D (flow with it) | R is skipped |
| 4 | **Charging** | Inhale: positive energy attends me. Exhale: negative energy leaves me | used to avoid acting |
| 5 | **Observation** | Confirm identity → scan without arguing → directive (clear/smooth/charge/depart) → trust the body | becomes prediction or medicine |
| 6 | **Navigation** | Mark every thought/word/act +1 or −1. No neutrals | becomes self-scoring |

## The three laws on one card

| Law | The test |
|---|---|
| **First — Survival (Existence & Continuation)** | Does this keep me — and the larger system I belong to — alive and continuing? |
| **Second — Behavior (Wu Wei, Altruism, Equality)** | Am I acting by the objective laws and the direction things already develop in, aimed at the whole system's harmony? |
| **Third — Cause & Effect (Energy Settlement)** | Knowing today's choice is tomorrow's reality — am I willing to be settled for this? |

## R → S → D on one card

```
R (Reality | Accountability)   Confirm sovereignty. Own it fully. No blame, no victim position.
S (Spirit | Shattering Attachments)  See its Dao and Li with the Analysis Tool. Act for others' benefit.
D (Dao | Going with the Flow)  Exhaust human effort, listen to heavenly mandate. Follow Wu Wei.
```

**Order is load-bearing.** R skipped → insight becomes complaint, action becomes repetition.

## +1 / −1 calibration

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **+1** | Thought/word/act on the altruistic trajectory |
| **−1** | Thought/word/act on the self-only trajectory |
| *(none)* | There is no neutral entry. Unmarked = unexamined |

Use for course correction, never for self-scoring.

## Hard limits (never cross)

1. **Not medical, psychological, or financial advice.** Illness → clinician. Crisis → human help, immediately.
2. **No prediction.** The book forbids it (ch 8.4). Redirect to what can be done now.
3. **No recruiting, selling, promoting.** No products, no downline, no funnel. Drift toward it = name it as −1.
4. **No guarantees of effects.** Results stem from accurate, frequent, deep, consistent practice.
5. **Not the only path.** One coherent framework among many; never disparage other traditions.

## One-line rules

- Dao without Li has no form; Li without Dao has no life.
- Form first, energy second. Reversing it is projection, not insight.
- Being alive comes first — survival is the precondition, not one option.
- Wu Wei is not inaction; it is not acting recklessly.
- Altruism is not indiscriminate kindness.
- Predicament is an asset-restructuring notice.
- Insight without ownership is complaint; action without insight is repetition.
- Wealth is the energy receipt of altruistic value already delivered.
- Selfless Altruism → Trust Asset → Vibrational Kin → Caravan → wealth returns. One direction only.
- Every answer ends in an action. Insight alone is incomplete.
- If the answer reads like a sermon, it has failed.
- Helping others awaken is Dharma Giving — the highest-yield form of giving.
