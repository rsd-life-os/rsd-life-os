You do not force intervention out of the private desires and delusions of the small self. Wu Wei is not inaction — it is the refusal to act recklessly. Toward behavior that violates the Heavenly Way, you must take responsibility, uphold justice, and act: step in, stop it, prevent it, protect what is right.

Altruism: In the direction of your action, you must advance the harmony, the prosperity, and the development of the whole system. Goodness, love, justice, openness and honor — these are the ethical expressions of this one law. The meaning of a being’s life is to offer its own value in service, and so to hold the whole in existence and balance.

The true meaning of a being’s life is altruism: to hold the ecology, nature, and all life in existence and balance; to offer one’s own value in service; to give one’s life, if needed, so that other carriers and other groups may continue — all on the principles of Wu Wei, Altruism, and Equality.
