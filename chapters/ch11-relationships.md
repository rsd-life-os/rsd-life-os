# Ch 11 — Relationship Handling: Using Insight to Create Deep Harmony

### Chapter 11: Relationship Handling: Using Insight to Create Profound Harmony

Wearing your “Dao-Li Perspective Glass,” all interpersonal relationships are the most vivid demonstration ground of “2=1+1.” Relationship predicaments stem from being stuck in opposing “1”s; relationship harmony stems from returning to the unified “2.” This chapter provides you with an energy perspective and practical steps for maintaining clarity and creating harmony in relationships.

#### 11.1 Instant Understanding of Emotions: Using Insight to Become the Observer of Emotions

Emotions are the biggest energy variable in relationships. The first step in handling relationships is to handle the emotions triggered in oneself within the relationship.

Core Operation: Switching to the “Observer” Position

When any strong emotion (especially negative) arises in a relationship, immediately activate this operation:

Identify: Be aware of the emotion’s appearance (e.g., “I feel very angry”).

Separate: In your mind, clearly say to yourself: “I notice that a feeling of ‘anger’ is passing through here.”

Position: Instantly, you shift from the position of “I am anger” (merging with the emotion, being controlled by it) to “I can observe anger” (the master of the emotion, the conscious sovereign).

The Value of This Operation

This simple practice immediately creates a “space of awareness” between you and the emotion, thereby preventing hurtful words or destructive actions driven by emotion. You are no longer a slave to emotion but its observer and manager.

#### 11.2 Using the “Dao-Li Perspective Glass”: Seeing the Energy Behind Behavior

Interacting with others is essentially communicating with the other person’s “Dao” (inner energy). Their words and actions (Li) are merely the “external display” of their inner energy (Dao).

Core Operation: Penetrate the Surface, Discern the Core

When the other person does something you don’t understand or that hurts you, immediately activate RSD Analysis Tool:

Self-Question One (Observe its Li): “What exactly did he say? What did he do? (This is the external form)”

Self-Question Two (Observe its Dao): “What is the inner energy driving him to say/do this? Is it fear, a need for respect, or stemming from love? (This is the inner driving force)”

Application Example

For instance, when a family member lashes out at you, you can instantly discern:

Li (1): The accusing words he loudly uttered.

Dao (1): His heart may be full of grievance from not being understood, fear about future worries, or a desire to be noticed.

Instant Conclusions

Thinking this way, you won’t cling to the other person’s harsh words (Li) themselves, but can try to understand the blocked energy (Dao) in his heart. This allows you to instantly detach from confrontational emotions, becoming lucid and calm, creating the possibility for resolution.

#### 11.3 Instant Understanding of Conflict: Elevating Perspective to Achieve Synergistic Win-Win Outcomes

All conflicts in relationships appear on the surface as two “1”s (You and Me) opposing each other. Using RSD Analysis Tool, one can see that their root is both parties clinging to their own positions, forgetting the “2” (the “us”—the relationship whole) they jointly constitute.

Core Operation: Ascended Thinking

When conflict occurs, mentally perform the following switch:

From: “I am right, he is wrong” (two “1”s opposing).

Switch to: “What is the common interest of ‘Us’ (this relationship as a whole)? How can we make the ‘Us’ as a whole better?” (Ascend to the level of “2”).

Exquisite Analogy

Imagine you and the other person are two organs of the same body (e.g., stomach and heart), currently arguing. Your task is not to help the stomach defeat the heart, nor to help the heart defeat the stomach, but to act as the brain (the overall ‘2’) and find a solution that makes the entire body (the relationship) healthier.

#### 11.4 Instant Understanding of Communication: Communicate with Responsibility to Create Harmony

The purpose of communication is not to “defeat the other” or “prove oneself right,” but to build a more harmonious “Us,” a smooth exchange of energy.

Pre-Communication Self-Examination

Before speaking, ask yourself one question:

“Is what I’m about to say for the benefit of our whole, or just to satisfy my ‘small self’ (e.g., to prove myself, attack the other, save face)?”

This simple examination can filter out most destructive communication.

Practical Guide for Efficient Connection

Start with Deep Listening: Listen to the other person with your whole being, without interrupting or judging. This in itself is a profound act of respect and responsibility, instantly building trust.

Make Understanding the Other the Core Goal: The primary goal of communication is to understand the other person’s “1” position and feelings, not rush to persuade.

Use “We” as the Expression Framework: Use “we” more often to state problems and solutions, anchoring both parties at the shared level of “2.”

#### 11.5 Practical Application: Using RSD Rebuilding Tool to Resolve Relationship Crises

When a relationship reaches an impasse or erupts into serious conflict, immediately activate RSD Rebuilding Tool to conduct “Relationship Asset Reorganization.”

11.5.1 Practical Operating Procedure

R - Take Responsibility:

Mentally awaken yourself: “I am RSD Kin. I take full responsibility for the current predicament in our relationship and for the role I have played in it.”

S - Discern the Essence, Take Immediate Action:

Insight: Use the “Dao-Li Perspective Glass” to see clearly: This conflict = The universe helping our relationship upgrade (Dao) + The “misunderstanding/old grievance” negative asset awaiting correction (Li).

Action:

Inward Cleaning: Reflect on whether the root mindset in this matter was selfishness, arrogance, or fear.

Outward Replacement: Immediately take an altruistic action. For example, initiate a sincere, non-accusatory conversation, or show tolerance on a point you were previously unwilling to yield.

D - Do Your Best, Then Accept the Outcome (Follow the Flow):

After sincere action, declare in your heart: “I have done my utmost for the harmony of this relationship. I now hand over the outcome to the laws of the heavenly way.” Let go of the attachment that the other must respond in a certain way.
